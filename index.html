The Three Laws
Universal grammar & phonetic rules
Law 1 — The 5-Vowel Cycle
Every vowel rotates one step forward so words remain fluid and 100% reversible:
A → E E → I I → O O → U U → A
Law 2 — Digraph Protection & H-Shift
Two-letter clusters SH and CH stay locked together, while standalone H shifts to X to prevent collisions.
Law 3 — Tokyo Orthography & Silent Vowels
Long vowel endings (such as -ou) are written in full orthography to preserve letter symmetry, while the trailing vowel is silent when spoken aloud.
